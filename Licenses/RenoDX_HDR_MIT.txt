RenoDX inverse tone mapping attribution
Source: https://github.com/clshortfuse/renodx/blob/main/src/shaders/inverse_tonemap.hlsl
Adapted: ReinhardScalable scalar equation (zero minimum/unit maximum) and
inverse BT.2446 Method A luma/chroma reconstruction.
Local changes: explicit input encoding selection, gray anchor expressed in nits,
finite peak cap for inverse Reinhard, safe black division, non-negative radicand
and bounded BT.2446A luma domain, shared neutral-ramp CPU/GPU scalar functions.
The rounded BT.2446A lower knee is held across its small gap so the inverse
luma ramp cannot step backwards at that branch boundary.
The old custom highlight-power curve is not used.

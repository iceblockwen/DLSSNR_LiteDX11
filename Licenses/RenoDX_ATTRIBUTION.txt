Lite HDR composition attribution
===============================
Source: Skynizz/optiscaler-dlss5, shader blob a17348b96569077b116ea71b36559307983d3bda
https://github.com/Skynizz/optiscaler-dlss5/blob/main/OptiScaler/shaders/dlssnr/precompile/dlssnr.hlsl
Its RenoDX attribution identifies clshortfuse's DLSS5 addon as the source of
UpgradeToneMap's two-branch highlight restoration, OkLab hue correction and
luminance/colour blending. These mechanisms are adapted in src/nr/shaders/lite_color.hlsl.
OkLab matrices are Bjorn Ottosson's published constants.
Lite retains its existing Hybrid input curve and Gain, uses an equal-luminance
BT.709 neutral for gamut compression, and explicitly anchors the final luminance
to the bounded original/model luminance ratio without a dark ratio floor.
This is not a claim of identical Krish v4.7 or ShortFuse binary behaviour.
